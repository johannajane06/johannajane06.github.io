Moriarty the Patriot or also known as Yuukoku no Moriarty is an anime series adapted from the manga of the same name. It portrays the story of William James Moriarty which is famously known as the archenemy of Sherlock Holmes. Instead of portraying him as a villain, the anime depicts William as a protoganist who fights against high class and aristocrats who abuses the lower class by using his intelligence and strategic thinking as the crime consultant.

The anime is set in the Victorian era in England and it explores a mixture of mystery, crime, social inequality, psychological thriller and action. As a fan of Sherlocck Holmes and mystery genre in general, I find this anime very intereguing and fascinating to watch. The plot of the anime is well written and the characters are also well developed and has their own unique personalities. I really like the dynamic between William and Sherlock Holmes in this anime because unlike the original story, I get to see them as allies and not as enemies and it is something that I never expected to see. As a fan of mystery genre and Sherlock Holmes I rate this anime a perfect 10/10⭐️ and I highly recommend it to anyone who likes mystery and crime genre.

Sore a Wife from Future is an Indonesian film that tells a story about Sore who went to the past to change her husband's fate from a tragic fate and lead him to a better life. Her husband, Jonanthan is a photographer who lives in Zagreb, Croatia is passionate about his work and is pursuing his dream to open his own photography gallery. One day, he woke up and found a woman who claims to be his wife from the future, Sore. This movie is a romantic, sci-fi, time travel movie that explores the themes of love, relationship, fate and the consequences of changing the past. I find this movie very heartcatching and emotional to watch and I can really feel the sadness and pain that the main character, Sore is going through in order to save her husband from the tragic fate that awaits him in the future.

Another aspect that catches my attention throughout the movie is the cinematography and the soundtrack in the movie. The cinematography in this movie really succesfully captures the beauty of Zagreb, Croatia and the beauty of the nature especially the focus on aurora phenomenon which is really captivating to watch. The soundtrack in this movie is also really good and every song that played for each scene really fit the mood and the vibe of the scene. One of my favourite soundtrack in this movie is the song "Terbuang dalam Waktu" by Barasuara which is played in the climax of the movie and its really emotional scene that really captures their relationship, meeting and all Sore's memories with Jonathan. I really like this movie and I highly recommend it to anyone who likes romantic, sci-fi and time travel movies. I rate this movie a perfect 10/10⭐️ This film is really a masterpiece and it shows that south asian cinema can produce really good films that can compete with the hollywood films.

Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba-The Movie: Infinity Castle is the direct sequel of the anime fourth season, Demon Slayer:Kimetsu no Yaiba-Hashira Training Arc. The movie shows the situations after the death of Kagaya Ubuyashiki, the leader of the Demon Slayer Corps and his wife and daughter which was killed by Muzan Kibutsuji the main antagonist of the series. Muzan retreats into the Infinity Castle which is his secret hideout and he also traps Tanjiro, his friends and the remaining Hashira in the castle and they found themselves being attacked by the lower rank demons and the encounter with the upper rank demons. The movie focuses on the battle between Shinobu Kocho, the insect Hashira and Doma, the upper rank 2 demon, the battle between Agatsuma Zenitsu, the thunder breathing user and his former senior Kaigaku, the upper rank 6 demon and the battle between Kamado Tanjiro, the main protagonist and Tomioka Giyu, the water Hashira against Akaza, the upper rank 3 demon. Apart from the battle, the movie also shows the backstory of Akaza before he become a demon which changes the perpective of the viewers about him and it also shows his redemption and the return of his memories with his master and his fiance, Koyuki.

I really find this movie epic and emotional to watch. As usual the animation in this movie is top notch especially the battle scenes where we can see the complexity of the fight choreography and the beauty of the animation. They also add a little bit of humor in the movie to put a little bit of lightheartdness in the movie. The movie also serves the viewers with amazing soundtrack that adds emotion and vibe in every scene in the movie. One of my favourite soundtrack in this movie is Akaza's Love theme which is played in the scene that shows the relationship between Akaza and his beloved fiance, Koyuki. It literally gives me goosebumps and not gonna lie, I cried so much during their scene because of how sad and tragic their love story is 🥹 and until now I still remember that scene vividly. I really like this movie and I highly recommend it to anyone who is a fan of the Demon Slayer series and also to anyone who likes action, fantasy and emotional movies. I rate this movie a perfect 10/10⭐️
